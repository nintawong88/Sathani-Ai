Adopt — clear wins, fit our locked decisions

Fifteen items. Each one is checked against the decision register before it's stamped.

ADOPT
A1
Robin Hood monetization frame — free for individuals forever; institutions pay for dashboards, customisation, reporting.
ChatGPT · Kimi

We had no revenue model at all. This one funds the mission without paywalling learners. "Your payment isn't tuition, it's the service layer + funding free access" — Kimi's FAQ framing answers the obvious objection.

Effort: positioning only — 0 build
ADOPT
A2
Founder story + Operations AI Blueprints page
Kimi (story) · Gemini (blueprints)

The single most defensible asset. Nobody else teaching AI in Thailand has run a 25,000-pallet ASRS or built an AI procurement bot. Gemini's blueprint cards (SAP eWM variance prompt, cold-chain vision-AI work order, reorder requisition) ARE the B2B lead magnet — and the content already exists from PIC work.

Effort: 1 page + 6–10 blueprint cards
ADOPT
A3
Institutional pricing table in THB
Kimi

Concrete anchors (100 seats = ฿48k/yr etc.) turn "corporate patronage" from a wish into a sellable SKU. Numbers need validating, but publishing a price is itself a filter.

Effort: 1 page
ADOPT
A4
White-label for libraries, senior centres, gov agencies
Kimi

This is the distribution answer for Second Act that we never solved — the learners are IN those buildings. Our facilitator kit becomes the delivery vehicle.

Effort: contract template + branding layer, phase 3

A5
Impact loop messaging — "every baht from institutions funds free learners"
Kimi · Gemini

Free to say, powerful for CSR-motivated Thai corporates, and true under A1.

Effort: copy only

Section 2 · Filed

Adapt — good idea, wrong shape

Take the core, change the mechanism to fit the decision register.

ADAPT
B1
Certificates / micro-badges ($15–49, LinkedIn-shareable)
Gemini · ChatGPT · Kimi

Yes for Builders and Operators only — a completion certificate tied to the capstone (deployed agent / measured automation), which makes it evidence, not a participation trophy. Never for Second Act or Adaptive Pace. Free with capstone; charge institutions, not learners.

ADAPT
B2
AI mentor / coach per learner
ChatGPT · Gemini

Not in MVP (locked decision: no LLM widget = no moderation surface, no minor exposure, no cost). Adapt as phase 3, Operators + Builders only, 20+, scoped to "help me apply this station to my task." Never for minors, never companionship-framed.

ADAPT
B3
AI Playground / sandbox
ChatGPT · Gemini

The copy-button prompts in our station pages already are a guided playground. A free-form sandbox for Sprouts contradicts our curated-cards-only child safety rule. Adapt: add 2–3 alternate prompts per Try step instead.

ADAPT
B4
Business Owners tier (7th tier)
ChatGPT

Real audience in Thailand (SME owners aren't "office workers"), but a 7th line dilutes MVP. Adapt as an Operators track ("เจ้าของกิจการ SME") in phase 2 — same stations, different examples.

ADAPT
B5
Learning-style dimension (read / watch / do / listen)
ChatGPT

Don't add a 4th routing question — our stations already contain all modes (Watch video, read Try, do Make). Adapt: make the transcript/audio option more prominent instead.

ADAPT
B6
Employer-sponsored badges → talent pool
Gemini · ChatGPT · Kimi

We rejected placement/matching for licensing-risk reasons — that stands. The compliant middle: employers may sponsor certification costs and browse the opt-in public showcase. No matching, no fees per placement, no intermediary role.

ADAPT
B7
Peer hubs / community forums
Gemini · ChatGPT

Correct instinct, ruinous moderation cost — and a segregated kids' forum is a child-safety surface we refuse. Adapt: facilitator community only (teachers, librarians, HR) in LINE OpenChat, phase 3.

ADAPT
B8
Supabase + RLS schema, full auth
Gemini

Good engineering for a different design. Conflicts with our no-accounts MVP and facilitator-only data model. Adopt Supabase when facilitator accounts arrive (phase 3) — our decision register already says exactly this. RLS pattern is worth stealing then.

ADAPT
B9
"We're hiring" section — writers, community managers, institutional partners, patrons
Kimi (TH About page)

Right idea, wrong timing: listing roles before launch signals scale that doesn't exist. Adapt: fold the institutional partner and patron asks into the Patron page now; publish writer/community roles only after the pilot produces its impact report (A7).

ADAPT
B10
Prompt Trainer — prompt scored on four stars (Clarity, Context, Format, Tone) with suggested improvements
ChatGPT (30-pg PDF, pp. 10–11, 19–20)

The best interactive-learning mechanic in any of the twelve sources, but the LLM version breaks two locked decisions. Adapt in two stages: MVP — the same four-part rubric as a static self-check under every Operators/Builders Try step (exactly the OP2 prompt anatomy already in our video script); Phase 3 — the AI-scored version for 20+, Operators/Builders only.

ADAPT
B11
Knowledge-graph navigation — explore related topics instead of a fixed sequence
ChatGPT (30-pg PDF, p. 20)

Wrong for beginners, who are precisely the people who don't know what to explore next — linear stations are a feature for five of our six lines. Adapt as a small "related stations" footer link between lines, phase 2. No graph UI.

ADAPT
B12
Workflow & Prompt Template Marketplace
Gemini (Execution Plan §4)

A marketplace means payments, seller vetting, and quality moderation — infrastructure rejected elsewhere. Adapt as a curated, free Blueprint Library — the Operations AI Blueprints page (A2) grown over time with community-submitted prompts behind a review gate. Same value, no commerce layer.

Section 2 · Filed

Reject — considered and declined, with reasons on record

Nothing here is dismissed silently. Ten items, each with the specific conflict that sank it.

REJECT
C1
Talent database / recruitment marketplace (฿5k–15k per placement)
ChatGPT · Kimi

Recruitment-agency licensing territory in Thailand + fraud-vetting burden. Locked decision #8 stands. B6 captures the safe portion.

REJECT
C2
Daily missions, streaks, gamification
ChatGPT

Actively harmful for Adaptive Pace and Second Act; mild value elsewhere. Capstone-as-proof is our engagement mechanic.

REJECT
C3
AI-as-companionship content for retirees ("using ChatGPT for companionship")
ChatGPT tier 3

Directly contradicts our Second Act design — we route toward family, buddies, and community, not toward the chatbot. This is the one place another plan is not just different but wrong.

REJECT
C4
Sponsored tool placements inside lesson content
Gemini

Tool recommendations must stay trustworthy — a paid slot inside a lesson poisons the well, especially for fraud-adjacent Second Act content. Sponsors buy patron branding (A5), never curriculum placement.

REJECT
C5
Multiple niche domains (kidsai.com etc.)
ChatGPT (raised & rejected there too)

One platform, one brand. Already our architecture; the interchange feature is the payoff.

REJECT
C6
Proctored exams
ChatGPT

Proctoring = surveillance infrastructure + PDPA surface for zero learner value. Capstone review does the job.

REJECT
C7
Individual "Pro" paid tier (personalized roadmap, AI mentor, certificates behind a paywall)
ChatGPT (30-pg PDF, pp. 21–22)

Conflicts with the Robin Hood model — and notably, ChatGPT's own later 87-page conversation dropped it in favour of free-for-individuals with B2B patronage, as did Kimi. The evolution across the source documents is itself the evidence. Free for individuals stays absolute.

REJECT
C8
Promised time savings per tier ("Expected time saved: 10 hours/week")
ChatGPT (30-pg PDF, p. 5)

An unverifiable marketing promise — the exact thing our OP4 script teaches learners not to do. We keep the mechanism (the hours-saved calculator produces the learner's own measured number) and quote real aggregated pilot results after the fact, not projections before it.

REJECT
C9
Paid gated talent-pool access ($1,000–$5,000/yr for employer access to verified portfolios)
Gemini (Execution Plan §6)

Re-creates the intermediary position rejected in C1, just priced annually instead of per placement. Our showcase stays public and free; employers may sponsor certification costs — access to people is never the product.

REJECT
C10
"AI Learning Foundation" multi-product umbrella (7 products incl. a marketplace and government portal)
ChatGPT (final chat)

Rejected for now, not forever — the seductive version of scope inflation: seven products sketched before one learner has finished one station. Directly violates A14's own filter. The ambition lives as one paragraph in VISION.md; everything else describes exactly one product until v1.0 ships.

ChatGPT's argument is the sharpest: "a roadmap is static; a compass helps people continuously navigate" — which lines up exactly with our AI GPS framing (A11) and the transit-map architecture. Three of its five phases are already complete here.
