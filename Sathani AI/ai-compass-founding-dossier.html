Part I · VISION.md

Vision, Mission & Model

วิสัยทัศน์ พันธกิจ และโมเดลธุรกิจ
วิสัยทัศน์ · Vision

Every Thai person, at every age, using AI confidently and safely

TH

คนไทยทุกคน ทุกวัย ใช้ AI ได้อย่างมั่นใจและปลอดภัย — โดยไม่มีค่าใช้จ่ายเป็นกำแพง

EN

Every Thai person, at every age, using AI confidently and safely — with cost never being the wall.

The gap we close is not between Thailand and Silicon Valley. It is between what AI can do and what Thai people believe they can do — ช่องว่างไม่ใช่ระหว่างประเทศไทยกับซิลิคอนวัลเลย์ แต่ระหว่าง สิ่งที่ AI ทำได้ กับ สิ่งที่คนไทยเชื่อว่าตัวเองทำได้

พันธกิจ · Mission

Six commitments

TH

เราสร้างเส้นทางเรียนรู้ AI ที่เข้ากับชีวิตจริงของผู้เรียน — เด็ก 7 ขวบ นักเรียน คนทำงาน เจ้าของ SME ผู้ที่มีพลังงานจำกัด และวัยเกษียณ — ฟรีสำหรับบุคคลตลอดไป โดยให้องค์กรเป็นผู้สนับสนุน สอนความปลอดภัยก่อนความสามารถ และวัดผลด้วยสิ่งที่ผู้เรียนสร้างได้จริง

  1. Six lines, one destination. Every learner gets a route matched to their life-stage and dose, not their technical background. All lines end at the same terminus: confident, safe, real-world use.
  2. Free for individuals is absolute. No individual paywall, ever. Institutions pay for the service layer — dashboards, customisation, reporting — and every baht funds free access. (Robin Hood model, A1/C7.)
  3. Safety before capability. Fraud defence at Station 2 for seniors; academic-integrity gates for teens; data-policy gates for professionals; no accounts, no records, no data for minors. Non-negotiable and non-skippable.
  4. Proof, not promises. No streaks, no XP, no projected time savings. The engagement mechanic is the capstone: a story printed for a family, an automation with a measured before/after, a deployed agent.
  5. Built from the factory floor. The curriculum draws on 28 years of real Thai operations — a 25,000-pallet ASRS, a ฿280M logistics budget, seven working apps at Pattana Intercool. เคสจริง งบจริง ทีมจริง.
  6. Thai first, world-class. ภาษาไทยก่อน, English second. Thai cases, Thai context, Thai typography, PDPA by design.
หลักการชี้นำ · The feature filter

One sentence, applied to everything

"If a feature doesn't help someone learn, apply, or confidently use AI — or keep them safe while doing so — it doesn't belong."

"ถ้าฟีเจอร์ใดไม่ได้ช่วยให้คนเรียนรู้ นำไปใช้ หรือใช้ AI ได้อย่างมั่นใจ — หรือช่วยให้เขาปลอดภัยระหว่างทาง — ฟีเจอร์นั้นไม่ควรอยู่ในแพลตฟอร์มนี้"

Already rejected on this filter, with reasons on record: gamified streaks, proctored exams, recruitment marketplaces, individual paywalls, sponsored placements inside lessons, AI-as-companionship for seniors, and a seven-product umbrella before one learner finished one station. Companion rule (A17): no document over 10 pages unless it is actively being used.

โมเดลธุรกิจ · The model

In one paragraph

Individuals learn free, anonymously, forever. Institutions — companies, schools, libraries, senior centres, government agencies — pay through a patronage ladder (community → gold) for team roadmaps, progress dashboards, white-label delivery, and patron recognition, at published THB prices. Employers may sponsor capstone certificates (Builders/Operators only) but never buy access to learners: no matching, no placement, no talent marketplace. Grants and vendor developer credits carry Phase 1.

If v1.0 succeeds — measured by the pilot impact report, not by this paragraph — the same architecture can carry school cohorts, government digital-literacy programmes, and deeper professional tracks. That ambition lives here and nowhere else until then. One product, one map, until the first learners finish the line.

8How many learners reported a concrete life outcome? · ผลลัพธ์ชีวิตจริงกี่รายVoluntary follow-up: a job application sent, an automation still running at 90 days, a scam recognised and avoided, a story given to a family
9Did anyone come to harm through the platform? · มีใครได้รับอันตรายหรือไม่Incident log — published even when the answer is uncomfortable. A platform teaching seniors and children owes an honest harm ledger, not only a highlight reel
OKR แรก · First OKRs

Set from Phase-0 demand data, not invented today

Objective: become a platform Thai learners and institutions trust with their first steps into AI. Each key result must map to one of the nine questions and be achievable on the realistic timeline (A18) — a small true number over a large hopeful one, in our metrics exactly as in our teaching. Only one KR exists today, because it is already a commitment: KR3 — one pilot delivered with a published impact report (100 learners, 5 facilitators, per A7). KR1 (completions), KR2 (active facilitators), and KR4 (first patron signed) are filled at Phase-1 launch from real signup data.

What we refuse to count as success: page views · time-on-site · streak lengths · signup counts without completions · press mentions · follower counts · any number that grows while a learner learns nothing. If the answers to the nine questions stop improving, the plan changes — not the questions.

Section 4

Naming — resolved by vote

AI Compass · เข็มทิศ AI — locked Rev 6, rejected on real-world check AI Navigator — Gemini lean, rejected on real-world check Pathwise AI + เข็มทิศ tagline — Kimi vote, rejected on real-world check Sathani AI · สถานี AI — working name, Rev 7

The review round's vote (Z.AI: Compass · Gemini: Navigator · Kimi: Pathwise hybrid · ChatGPT: postpone) picked AI Compass / เข็มทิศ AI 3–1. A founder search-check then found it already operated by a funded Antwerp AI-consulting firm — enterprise clients, a government-registered subsidy status, and the aicompass.ai domain. The fallback, AI Navigator, collided worse: both Accenture and Deloitte launched flagship enterprise-AI platforms under that exact name in 2025–2026. The next fallback, Pathwise AI, is a live App Store app with its domain already registered.

Three real-world rejections in a row revealed a pattern no amount of internal voting could have caught: the entire English "AI + navigation-metaphor" naming space — Compass, Navigator, Pathwise, and likely Pathfinder or Wayfinder next — was claimed by the 2025–2026 AI-naming rush before this project reached the market. The fix was to stop competing in that space.

Sathani AI · สถานี AI — "AI Station" — names the product's actual mechanism instead of a borrowed metaphor: every lesson in this project is already called a station. Thai script leads per Mission Commitment #6. An initial search returned zero collisions in either script — no company, app, or product carries this name. Two checks remain, both the founder's: domain availability, and a DBD trademark search on both scripts.
