## ทำไมต้องมีเอกสารนี้ · Why this file exists

The register defines what we build, how it's funded, and what keeps learners safe. It never defined what winning looks like. This file does — and it is answered **annually, in public**, on the /journal page.

## คำถามประจำปี · The annual questions

| # | Question · คำถาม | Measured how |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How many people started a line? · มีกี่คนเริ่มเส้นทาง | line_selected events |
| 2 | How many completed all four stations? · มีกี่คนเรียนจบครบสี่สถานี | station_complete on station 4 |
| 3 | How many capstones were actually made? · มีชิ้นงานจริงเกิดขึ้นกี่ชิ้น | Showcase submissions + facilitator reports (many capstones are private by design — a printed family story is a success we may never count; note undercounting honestly) |
| 4 | How many facilitators ran sessions? · ผู้ดูแลจัดการเรียนกี่คน | Facilitator accounts + kit downloads |
| 5 | How many schools, libraries, and senior centres delivered a line? · สถาบันกี่แห่ง | Pilot + white-label agreements |
| 6 | How many companies became patrons, at which ladder tier? · องค์กรอุปถัมภ์กี่ราย | Signed patron agreements |
| 7 | How many free learners did each patron baht fund? · เงินอุปถัมภ์ 1 บาท สนับสนุนผู้เรียนฟรีกี่คน | Patron revenue ÷ active free learners |
| 8 | How many learners reported a concrete life outcome? · ผลลัพธ์ชีวิตจริงกี่ราย | Voluntary follow-up: a job application sent, an automation still running at 90 days, a scam recognised and avoided, a story given to a family |
| 9 | Did anyone come to harm through the platform? · มีใครได้รับอันตรายหรือไม่ | Incident log — published even when the answer is uncomfortable |

Question 9 is listed deliberately. A platform teaching seniors and children owes its funders and its learners an honest harm ledger, not only a highlight reel.

## OKR แรก · First OKRs (set at Phase 1 launch, reviewed quarterly)

**Objective:** become a platform Thai learners and institutions trust with their first steps into AI.

Key results are set from Phase-0 demand data, not invented here. The rule for setting them: each KR must map to one of the nine questions above, and each must be achievable by the realistic timeline (A18) — a small true number over a large hopeful one, in our metrics exactly as in our teaching (OP4).

## สิ่งที่เราจะไม่นับเป็นความสำเร็จ · What we refuse to count as success

Page views. Time-on-site. Streak lengths. Signup counts without completions. Press mentions. Follower counts. Any number that grows while a learner learns nothing.
