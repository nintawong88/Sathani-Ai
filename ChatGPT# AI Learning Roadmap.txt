# AI Learning Roadmap

## Vision

Build an AI learning platform that adapts to each person's lifestyle, age, occupation, goals, and available learning time.

Instead of asking:

> "What AI tool should I learn?"

The platform asks:

- Who are you?
- What do you want to achieve?
- How much time do you have?

Then generates a personalized AI roadmap.
