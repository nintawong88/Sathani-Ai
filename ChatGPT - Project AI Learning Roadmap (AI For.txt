2. Audience Tiers & Curriculum Strategy
Instead of standard "Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced" tiers, users are grouped by lifestyle and cognitive bandwidth.

Tier 1: AI Sprouts (Kids & Young Teens)
Profile: Ages 7-14. High curiosity, short attention span.
Curriculum Focus: What is AI? How does it learn? Using AI for homework (ethically), creating art with AI.
Format: Embedded YouTube videos, interactive puzzles, cartoon guides, sandbox play areas.
Tier 2: AI Navigators (College Students & Office Workers)
Profile: Ages 16-50. Goal-oriented, busy, looking for efficiency.
Curriculum Focus: Prompt engineering, automating daily tasks, AI for data analysis, workplace AI ethics. Includes modules based on Chawin's enterprise app experience (e.g., "How to Build an AI Procurement System").
Format: Video tutorials, text guides, curated external links, workflow templates.
Tier 3: AI Companions (Retirees & Individuals with Health/Low-Energy Needs)
Profile: Seniors, chronically ill, limited tech background, potential visual/motor impairments.
Curriculum Focus: Voice assistants (Alexa/Siri), using AI to read aloud, identifying AI scams/deepfakes, using ChatGPT for companionship.
Format: Audio-first modules, large-print text, simple interfaces, "pause and resume anytime" structure.
